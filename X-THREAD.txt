Every week someone posts a faster Qwen3.8-27B on an RTX 5090. 320 tok/s, 270, 220…

The question almost nobody answers: do you get the same reply when a second person hits the server at the same time?

We built for that. Here's the public field next to ours 🧵

2/
Most fast demos cut a corner: a meter that skips prefill, replies capped short, or answers that drift under load.

Our bar for "lossless":
• speculative output = plain greedy of the same 4-bit checkpoint, token for token
• a 2nd in-flight request can't flip a token

Last check: 16/16, incl. 4-wide.

3/
That second rule costs real speed. Batch-invariant kernels drop plain greedy to ~38 tok/s. Most 5090 setups run plain at ~85–115.

So we buy it back with DFlash2 K=7: draft 7, verify 8 in one pass, keep only what the 27B would have said anyway. ~5.67 tokens kept per step.

5/
Ours, locked 4 Nov. Wall clock with prefill, replies not cut, 57k window:

Thinking off: 290.5 tok/s alone → 614.3 with 4 at once
Thinking on: 228.7 → 467.4

Four users, each getting exactly the reply they'd get alone. That's the product, not the peak.
